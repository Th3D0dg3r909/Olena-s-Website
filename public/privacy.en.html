Privacy statement

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Who is responsible?

Olena Skin Care Studio, Doezastraat 31, 2311 HA Leiden, Chamber of Commerce (KvK) 94229538, is responsible for processing your personal data through this website. For questions about privacy, call 06 47238444 or send a WhatsApp message.

What data do we use?

You request an appointment by sending Olena a WhatsApp message. That message, with your name, phone number and whatever you write in it, is handled through WhatsApp (Meta) and can be seen in Olena’s WhatsApp. The website itself does not store your message. When Olena confirms your appointment, she enters your name, phone number, the treatment and the date and time in the studio’s appointment planner on this website. We use this data only to plan and carry out your appointment and to contact you about it. The legal basis is performing the appointment you make with us (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

How long do we keep your data?

Bookings are deleted automatically 12 months after the appointment date.

Who do we share data with?

We do not sell your data or share it for marketing. The website runs at a hosting company (Hostinger), which stores the data on our behalf.

The reviews on this website come from Google Maps. To show them, your browser loads reviewers’ profile photos directly from Google, so Google receives your IP address. Links to WhatsApp, Instagram and Google Maps open services of those companies, and their own privacy terms apply. A message you type in the chat window is not stored by this website: it is only sent when you send it yourself in WhatsApp.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies for visitors and does not use analytics or tracking services. Only logging in to the studio’s appointment planner uses a strictly necessary session cookie.

Your rights

You may view, correct or delete your data. You may also object to its use, have its use restricted and request a copy to give to someone else. To do so, call 06 47238444 or send a WhatsApp message; we reply within one month. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
